# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a shared space for people building and reviewing tools. Be
respectful, be patient with newcomers, and assume good intent. Critique the work,
not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give clear, specific feedback on code and docs.
- Credit others when you reuse their ideas.
- Say when you are not sure, and ask questions in the open.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in any channel.
- Publishing someone's private details without their consent.
- Deliberately disrupting issues, reviews or discussions.

## Reporting

Report a concern through GitHub private vulnerability reporting for conduct that
involves privacy or safety, or by opening a private discussion with the maintainers
on this repository. Reports are read in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and contributions that break
this code. Repeated or serious breaches lead to a ban from the project's spaces.
